Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@holdone.ro. We read every message.

How does Voice mode work?

Open a script and press play in Voice mode, then start reading aloud. The app listens through the microphone, recognizes your words on the device, and keeps the line you are reading in the light. When you pause, it waits. If you skip ahead or ad-lib, tap a word to bring the light back to it.

Prefer not to use your voice? Auto scrolls at a steady speed and Manual lets you move the script yourself.

Tips

  • Speak at your normal pace; there is no need to over-enunciate.
  • Parakeet, the default engine for English, is the fastest. For other languages, choose a language in Settings and the Apple engine follows it.
  • If the light lags or jumps, try the Sensitivity setting (Careful, Normal, Eager).

What is free, and what is Pro?

The whole app is free for scripts up to 600 spoken words, in every mode. Voice mode on a longer script needs Pro. Auto and Manual stay free at any length.

Pro is a single lifetime purchase: one payment, no subscription. It is tied to your Apple Account, so it works on your other iPhones and iPads too.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open a script longer than 600 words in Voice mode. On the Pro screen, tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

The app can’t hear me

Voice mode needs microphone access, and the Apple engines also need speech recognition access. If you said no at first, open the iOS Settings app, go to Apps › AnyPrompter, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Recognition happens on your device; see the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are my scripts?

On your device, in the app. You can paste text or import plain text, RTF and PDF files. Scripts are not uploaded anywhere, so deleting the app deletes them unless you have a device backup.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we are happy to help with any problem first.
